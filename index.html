StaticEconomy
Question Summary
Statement I: Income from poultry farming and wool rearing in rural areas is exempt from tax. Statement II: Rural agricultural land is NOT a capital asset under Income Tax Act 1961. Answer: (b) Both I and II correct but II does not explain I
Type & Difficulty
Type: Static — Tax Law
Difficulty: Hard
Source: Income Tax Act 1961, Sections 2(14), 10(1), 10(2).
Books: Ramesh Singh Economy; Tax provisions chapter
Source of Topic — Static Background
Income Tax Act 1961, Section 2(14) defines "capital asset" — rural agricultural land located beyond specified distances from municipalities is NOT a capital asset, so its sale is not taxable as capital gains. Section 10(1) exempts agricultural income. Allied agricultural activities like poultry, dairy, wool production in rural areas are also exempt under the definition of agricultural income when integrated with farming.

This is a tricky static question. Statement II explains WHY rural agri land is not taxable (because it's not a capital asset) but this is not the reason why poultry income is exempt — poultry is exempt because it qualifies as allied agricultural activity.

Capital Receipts — Liabilities, Borrowings, Disinvestment, Interest
StaticEconomy
Question Summary
I: Capital receipts create liability or reduce assets. II: Borrowings and disinvestment are capital receipts. III: Interest received on loans creates govt liability. Answer: (a) I and II only — Statement III is wrong: interest received is REVENUE receipt, not capital. It doesn't create liability — it's income.
Type & Difficulty
Type: Static — Government Budget
Difficulty: Medium
Source: NCERT Class 12 Macro Chapter 5 (Government Budget); Ramesh Singh
Source of Topic — Static Background
Government Budget Classification: Capital receipts are receipts that either create a liability (borrowings — Govt has to repay) OR reduce an asset (disinvestment — Govt sells its shareholding). Revenue receipts are recurring and don't create liabilities (taxes, interest income, fees). Interest received on loans given by Govt is a REVENUE receipt because it's regular income, not a liability-creating receipt. NCERT Macro Chapter 5 covers this extensively.

Source of Topic — Static Background
Araghatta (also called Rahat or Persian Wheel): A water-lifting device used in ancient and medieval India. Consists of a large wheel with earthen pots attached to its rim/spokes. As the wheel rotates (driven by bullocks or water current), pots scoop water from the well and empty it into a trough at the top. Mentioned in texts like Arthashastra. Different from: (a) Picottah/Mot — leather bag pulled by pulley (different device), (d) Dhenkli — a simpler lever device.

Question Summary

Answer: (c) Statement I correct but Statement II is not correct — subtropical regions are actually drier (less rainfall = more dust in air). Statement II is wrong — subtropical areas have MORE dry winds (anticyclonic).
Type & Difficulty
Type: Static — Climatology
Difficulty: Hard
Source: GC Leong Atmosphere chapter; NCERT Class 11 Physical Geo Ch.9–10
Source of Topic — Static Background
Atmospheric Dust Distribution: Dust particles (aerosols) are more concentrated in subtropical (~30°N/S) and temperate areas because: subtropical zones have less rainfall (arid/semi-arid), more dry vegetation, more desert dust (Sahara, Arabian, Thar), and stronger winds carrying dust. Equatorial regions have heavy rainfall which washes dust from atmosphere. Polar regions have ice/snow which suppresses dust. Statement II claims subtropical areas have fewer dry winds — this is WRONG. Subtropical zones have descending anticyclonic air = dry winds (trade winds, westerlies originate here).
